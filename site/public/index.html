How Writ helps

01 / LID

No more silent microphone.

The built-in mic still appears when a MacBook is closed, even though the hardware cannot hear you. Writ knows the difference and skips it.

02 / METER

See the room, not just your voice.

Peak, rolling average, and noise floor reveal whether your setup is clean before the call or recording starts. Audio is measured, then discarded.

03 / AIRPLAY

AirPlay survives reconnection.

macOS gives speakers temporary identities. Name one once and Writ remembers it without taking you off a speaker you chose on purpose.

Your audio stays on your Mac

A microphone utility with no appetite for your data.

No account. No analytics. No crash reporting. The level meter runs only while the input panel is open, and no audio is recorded or transmitted. Automatic update checks make a standard web request, which exposes your IP address and standard headers to the server; they include no device identifier, audio settings, or in-app activity and can be turned off.
