Writ

Your audio devices, in the order you actually want them.

macOS has no concept of device priority — it switches to whatever connected most recently, every time. Writ is the standing order that says otherwise.

  • Knows the built-in mic is dead when the lid is shut, and skips it
  • Warns you when Bluetooth is quietly destroying your audio quality
  • Remembers your AirPlay speakers, which macOS does not
  • Mute from anywhere, with the menu bar showing it
